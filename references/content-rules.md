# Content rules

The director's rules from the Shepherdr production. They are not negotiable; when a brief asks for something that
breaks one, say so and offer the closest thing that doesn't.

1. **Parody and wink at references; never copy them.** No footage, lines, music or backgrounds lifted from the thing
   being parodied ("I don't want us to literally copy the backgrounds and lines"; "winks… not a remix"). Structure and
   attitude are fair game; the specific creative work is not.
2. **Nerdy humour, never sexualized.** No profanity. ("Let's be nerdier.")
3. **Real people:**
   - quotes only if they really said them, with their full name and title on screen, and the source and date in the
     script's Notes;
   - photos only under a licence that allows it (CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain, e.g. via Wikimedia Commons), as
     undistorted cut-outs, with the attribution in the video's description or on its end card (CC BY and CC BY-SA
     require it wherever the video is posted);
   - never their voice: no cloning a real person's voice without their written consent.
4. **Never show confidential screens.** Calendars, colleagues' names, client data, audit or financial figures, session
   names: anonymize or rebuild the screen before it appears (the Shepherdr explainer replaced an internal page with an
   anonymized version). Raw captures don't go into a repository that may ever be shared.
5. **Claudia** (claudia.gallery) is an open character anyone may use. Every video she appears in credits her exactly as
   "Claudia is an open character by @anabology · claudia.gallery". Her identity text comes from the claudia MCP's
   `claudia_canon`; the norms for releasing a song with her are at the end of it.
6. **Disclose AI-generated content**, on the end card and in the post: what was generated, with what (voices, music,
   effects, images, footage).
7. **On-screen text in English**, unless the brief says otherwise.
8. **Product claims are true or obvious hyperbole.** A number on screen has its source in the script's Notes.
9. **Third-party brands and products** appear as themselves only from their own press material, credited ("Image:
   Tesla, via Electrek"), and never imply endorsement. The same goes for logos and wordmarks: The Agile Monkeys'
   wordmarks in `engine/cutouts/` are its trademarks, not covered by the kit's open licences; use your own logo in your
   videos.
