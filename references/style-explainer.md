# The calm explainer style

The Shepherdr corporate explainer (the director: "looks perfect… perfect for the company's social channels"): for a
company's own channels, when the message must be understood rather than shouted. Same engine, same voice pipeline, a
quieter grammar.

- **Structure:** the problem in one line → the product → how it works, one idea per cut, each with a real (anonymized)
  screenshot → one or two numbers → the logo card.
- **Look:** dark background (#0a0d0c), Fira Code headlines typed in at the top with **one accent word** in green,
  screenshots in a window frame that the camera pans across, a green frame highlighting one region, chips, stat tiles.
- **Motion:** every cut dips in from black (0.3 s); things fade and rise; no punches, no shakes, no scanlines.
- **Voice:** a warm, clear narrator at 1.0×, clean chain, gaps of 0.25-0.7 s, a 1.5 s lead-in. No forced laughs or
  non-verbal tags: the tone carries it.
- **Music:** a warm intro, light drums on the reveal, a groove under the features, a resolved ending; 6 dB under the
  voice between phrases (the first explainer mix was inaudible at 14-20 dB under).
- **Screens:** real ones, anonymized first: no client data, colleagues' names, calendars or internal figures.
