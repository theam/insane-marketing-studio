# THE PRODUCT! — script v1 (infomercial)

A short late-night infomercial. Replace this whole file with the real script; keep the format.

**Tone tags used:** frantic, absurd, nerdy. **Length target:** 25 s.

**Format.** `## Script` holds the narration. `**N · Title**` starts a section: each section gets its own music style, so
start a new one where the story turns. A paragraph starting with `NAME:` (in capitals) is that speaker's; every other
paragraph is the narrator's. `[tags]` direct the delivery (Eleven v4: `[excited]`, `[shouting]`, `[calmly]`,
`[dramatically]`, `[fast]`, `[deep voice]`…); a tag holds until the next one. Words in CAPITALS are shouted and punch the
frame. `{#name}` marks the word an animation lands on; marks are stripped before the voice is generated. Spell names the
way the voice should say them (here, "Prodd-uct" would be wrong; keep plain words) and write the on-screen spelling in the
page.
