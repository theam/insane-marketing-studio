**Format.** The narration goes under the Script heading below. `**N · Title**` starts a section (each gets its own
music style). `{#name}` marks the word an animation lands on. In an explainer, don't force non-verbal tags (no
`[laughs]`): let the tone carry it (the director, on the Shepherdr explainer). Words in capitals are not shouted here.
